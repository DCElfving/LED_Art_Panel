About this project

[+]

A standalone, WiFi-enabled digital art frame driven by an ESP32 and a 32x32 RGB LED Panel. This project creates a low-resolution display (inspired by artist Jim Campbell) that plays static images and animated GIFs uploaded directly from your phone or computer.

  • Touchless Setup: Connects to your home WiFi or creates its own fallback Access Point.
  • Easy Discovery: Accessible via mDNS instead of memorizing IP addresses.
  • Browser-Side Processing: All resizing, cropping, and conversion happens privately in your browser.
  • Smart Playback: Smooth cross-fades and random shuffle playlist.
